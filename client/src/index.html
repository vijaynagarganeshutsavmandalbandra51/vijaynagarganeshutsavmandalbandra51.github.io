Vijay Nagarcha Raja – Vijay Nagar Sarvajanik Ganesh Utsav Mandal

विजय नगरचा राजा – विजय नगर सार्वजनिक गणेशोत्सव मंडळ, वांद्रे (पूर्व), मुंबई ४०००५१.

Our Sarvajanik Ganeshotsav mandal was started in 1969 by a mutual decision of the residents of Buildings 1 to 8 of Teacher's Colony, Vijay Nagar, near Cardinal Gracias High School, Bandra (East), Mumbai 400051. Besides Ganeshotsav the mandal also celebrates Gudi Padwa. Every year we hold an annual Bhandara open to everyone, serving 300–400 people. In 2026 our Kedarnath decoration, built by our own young volunteers, was featured on Jai Maharashtra News.
